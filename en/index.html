01 — The idea

The hard part isn't the data. It's getting someone to look at it.

Almost all business software dies the same way: bought with enthusiasm, half-loaded with data, and six months later only one person still opens it. The data exists. Nobody sees it.

We flip that around: the information goes looking for the person, through the one channel they actually keep open — nothing to install, nothing to learn.

That comes with a cost we'd rather state upfront: not everything should be automated. If what people ask changes every day and never repeats, FiBOT won't help you — and we'll say so in the first conversation.

What does work is the boring stuff: the question someone asks fourteen times a week, and the document that expires while nobody's watching.

04 — How it starts

How it starts, no detours.

Day 1 · 30 minutes

One conversation, and nothing more

You tell us what your team gets asked every day and how they handle it now. That's enough to tell whether this fits. If it doesn't, it ends right there and costs you nothing.

Weeks 1 and 2

One case running, not the whole system

We pick the question that repeats the most and connect it to what you already have — the usual system, the spreadsheet, whatever it is. Within two weeks there's something running with a small group of real people.

After that

What gets added is whatever use demands

The second case gets picked by the people using the first one, not by a planning meeting. What nobody asks for doesn't get built — and that's exactly where most projects blow their budget.

For anyone who wants to audit it

Here's how it's built, in four points.

Language

You write to it like you would to a person. No exact commands, no menu of options: you ask the way you'd ask a coworker, and the system figures out what's needed.

Integration

By API, not scraping. It connects to the systems you already use —ERP, spreadsheets, your own databases— through the API or a direct read. No shared credentials, no touching the original source.

Data

It reads, never writes. Read-only access to whatever gets authorized. It doesn't modify an existing system unless explicitly asked to, and that comes with its own separate permission.

Security

Role-based access, everything logged. Credentials and tokens never pass through the chat — they stay out of it, like in any serious integration. Every access gets logged: who, what, when.

Each of these four points goes into more detail the moment it's needed. The more you ask, the better.

05 — Questions

What people always ask us.

Do I have to switch systems?

No. We connect to whatever you already have — ERP, spreadsheets, GPS, anything. If yours isn't mapped yet, we look into it, but it isn't always possible — and we find that out in the first week, not six months in.

What if my team doesn't use it?

That's the right question to ask, and the reason this lives on WhatsApp instead of an app. Either way, we measure usage from day one: how many questions, from how many different people. If after four weeks only one person is writing in, the problem wasn't the tool, and we'll tell you that.

How much does it cost?

We don't publish a price list, because the first case defines the scope and the differences are big. What we can promise: the first conversation ends with a firm number, not a range or an "it depends."

Where does my data live?

In a database that belongs only to your company, separate from any other client's. No query from one ever touches another's data — and that's not a sales promise, they're simply separate projects.

And if I want to leave tomorrow?

You take your data, exported, and it shuts off. No lock-in, no long contract. We'd rather you stay because you're using it — which is also the only way this actually works out for us.

What repetitive task would you like to stop doing?

Tell us what your team gets asked every day. If it's always the same thing, FiBOT can probably answer it. If not, we'll tell you — selling something that won't work is bad business for both of us.
